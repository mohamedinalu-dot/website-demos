Questions

What can I order?

Radha’s social pages mention designer cakes, cupcakes, cake pops, doughnuts and cookies, plus party props and a candy cart to hire. Everything is made to order, so just ask what you have in mind.

How much does a cake cost?

That depends on what you’d like and the design. Send your request on WhatsApp and you’ll hear the price.

Do you make eggless or vegan cakes?

Eggless and vegan options are mentioned on Radha’s social pages. Ask on WhatsApp which designs work for you.

Can you take a dietary need into account?

Reviewers mention that Radha took lactose intolerance into account. Mention your dietary need in your message.

Can I order at short notice?

Reviewers mention last-minute orders. Send your date on WhatsApp and you’ll hear whether it’s possible.
